Friday the fourteenth of August

For Daria

the day, its light, and the world it lives in

The mood

Black tie in the City, understated and unhurried. What we want is a mix of natural moments with a few editorial looks: most of the day caught rather than staged, and a handful of properly composed frames where the building earns them.

We love detailed, fun photographs. Cufflinks and coupes, the stationery, rings against paper, laughter caught sideways, the walk rather than the arrival. If a picture has a joke hiding in it somewhere, better still.

Timeless over trend: nothing that will date the pictures faster than the dinner jackets will.

The light

The best fifteen minutes of the day; steal the two of us away in it.

Blue hour to about 8:55, then the Saloon by candlelight.

Details worth an eye

The blind-embossed C & A seal, on the stationery and everywhere the day is printed. The bar card and the table plate, engraved in the same hand. The menus. The petals. The cabs, black on black tie. The rings against the paper.

The couple's website carries a hand-drawn ink map of the cab route across London; the printed things at dinner are its family.

People and asks

Thirty guests, one room, no one to miss. A short family-groups list will follow from us directly.

Kindly no flash during the ceremony, and the couple would rather be stolen for ten perfect minutes than posed for forty.

We cannot wait to see it through your eyes.
